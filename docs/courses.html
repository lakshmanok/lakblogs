Teaching & Education

Making technical knowledge practical.

At Google Cloud, I led the development of the data analytics and machine learning training curriculum used by customers, partners, and Google employees. My focus was practical: helping people turn technical knowledge into the ability to solve real problems.

Earlier, at the University of Oklahoma, I taught courses in spatial programming and the automated analysis of spatial grids.

Building enterprise AI capability

Google Cloud’s Advanced Solutions Lab

I co-founded Google Cloud’s Advanced Solutions Lab, bringing enterprise teams together with Google’s machine learning experts to apply AI to real business problems.

The program combines immersive, hands-on learning with work on participants’ own business challenges—helping teams build the skills to develop and deploy AI solutions.
