Dr. Valliappa Lakshmanan is co-founder and CTO of Obin AI, where he sets the company’s technical and scientific direction. Obin builds increasingly autonomous AI agents for financial services, combining deep domain expertise with an open architecture designed for a regulated industry.

Previously, Lak was an Operating Executive at Silver Lake, partnering with portfolio-company leaders on data and AI strategy. As Director of Data Analytics and AI Solutions at Google Cloud, he led teams building solutions for enterprise business problems and co-founded Google’s Advanced Solutions Lab.

Earlier, at the University of Oklahoma and NOAA’s National Severe Storms Laboratory, Lak pioneered AI in severe weather forecasting and led the development of WDSS-II, whose algorithms have been used worldwide in government and industry.

Lak co-authored Generative AI Design Patterns and Machine Learning Design Patterns, among other books on AI and data. He is a Fellow of the American Meteorological Society and holds a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Oklahoma.
