Biography

From applied research to technology leadership.

I’m Dr. Valliappa Lakshmanan—most people call me Lak. I’m co-founder and CTO of Obin, where I set the company’s technical and scientific direction.

My career has taken me from pioneering AI in severe weather forecasting to building enterprise AI capabilities at Google Cloud, advising technology businesses at Silver Lake, and now building increasingly autonomous agents at Obin. Across those roles, I’ve focused on turning research and engineering into systems people can use.

Career

2025–present

Obin

Co-founder & CTO

I set Obin’s technical and scientific direction and lead the development of our product. We build increasingly autonomous agents for financial services using an open architecture designed for a regulated industry.

2022–2025

Silver Lake

Operating Executive

I partnered with the management teams of portfolio companies to grow their businesses through data and AI-driven innovation. I helped shape data and AI strategy and provided guidance on investments in public cloud, data analytics, and machine learning.

2016–2022

Google Cloud

Director of Data Analytics and AI Solutions · 2019–2022

I led a team building solutions for enterprise business problems, including Contact Center AI Insights, anomaly detection, MLOps, and data warehouse modernization.

In 2021, solutions developed by my team generated $800M+ in annual revenue.

Technical Lead · 2016–2019

I co-founded Google’s Advanced Solutions Lab, led the development of data and machine learning training, and helped create machine learning consulting practices. Through books, courses, certification, and hands-on engagements, I worked to make these capabilities accessible to more people and organizations.

In 2019, 300,000+ learners spent more than four hours on content developed by my team.

2014–2016

The Climate Corporation

Director of Meteorology

I led a team of statisticians, engineers, and meteorologists building weather inputs for agricultural analytics. Our work included rainfall estimates that supported farmers’ decisions. I set product and science roadmaps while remaining involved in hands-on data science and mentoring the team.

1995–2014

NOAA / University of Oklahoma

Senior Research Scientist

At the National Severe Storms Laboratory and the University of Oklahoma, I pioneered AI in severe weather forecasting. I led the development of WDSS-II, whose severe weather algorithms have been used worldwide in government and industry, and developed methods for real-time pattern recognition, large-scale data analysis, and weather prediction.

Books, research & teaching

I share what I’ve learned through books, teaching, and research. My books include Generative AI Design Patterns and Machine Learning Design Patterns, alongside work on data platforms, computer vision, and geospatial analysis.

Advisory roles & service

I’ve advised academic and scientific organizations and helped develop the next generation of researchers and practitioners.

  • University of Oklahoma: Gallogly College of Engineering Board of Advisors (2023–present) and College of Atmospheric and Geographic Sciences Board of Advisors (2025–present).
  • Washington State University: advisory role for the bachelor’s program in Data Analytics (2017–present).
  • Allen Institute for Immunology: Science Advisory Board (2021–2022).
  • Conrad Blucher Institute for Surveying and Science: advisory board (2017–2022).

I’ve also advised graduate students, chaired the American Meteorological Society’s Artificial Intelligence Committee, and helped organize Climate Informatics conferences and machine learning competitions.
